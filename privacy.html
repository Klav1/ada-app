ADA privacy policy

Effective 28 September 2026.

Who uses ADA

ADA is a personal assistant with exactly one user: its developer. It is not published, sold or offered to anyone else.

What Google data ADA accesses

With the user's consent, through the Google Data Portability API: his YouTube activity, his Google Search activity and his Chrome browsing history. ADA requests nothing else from Google.

How the data is used

Only to give the user summaries and reminders about his own activity, and to answer his own questions about it. It is not used for advertising, profiling for anyone else, or training general-purpose AI models.

Where it is stored

On computers the user owns (a home server and his laptop), transferred between them over an encrypted private network.

Sharing

The data is not sold or shared with anyone. Short summaries may be processed by an AI model provider (Anthropic) to produce the user's own daily summary; the result is delivered only to the user.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the user deletes it. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the support email shown on ADA's Google sign-in screen.
